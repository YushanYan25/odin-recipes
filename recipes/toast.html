Avocado Toast

Description: Avocado toast is an open-faced sandwich made of toasted bread topped with mashed avocado, salt, and various seasonings

Ingredients

  • Two slices of toasted bread
  • One large avocado
  • Fresh lemon juice
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Red pepper flake
  • Sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Gently mash peeled and pitted avocado with lemon juice
  2. Divide and spread mashed avocado across the two slices of bread
  3. Dizzle each slice of toast with olive oil and sprinkle salt, pepper, red pepper flakes and sesame seeds
