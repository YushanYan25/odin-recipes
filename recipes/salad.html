Chef Salad

Description: Chef salad is an American salad consisting of hard-boiled eggs, one or more varieties of meat, tomatoes, cucumbers, and cheese, all placed upon a bed of tossed lettuce or other leaf vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Iceberg lettuce
  • Boston Bibb lettuce
  • Cucumber
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Turkey, Ham, Roast beef
  • Swiss and Cheddar cheese
  • Hard boiled eggs
  • Avocado
  • Thousand island dressing

Instructions

  1. Prepare the lettuces
  2. Prep and cut all the toppings
  3. Assemble the salad
  4. Add dressing and mix
