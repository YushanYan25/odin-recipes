Cupcake

DescriptionA vanilla cupcake that with a plush, tender crumb like you get at high-end patisseries that will stay perfectly fresh for 4 days

  • All purpose flour
  • Eggs
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Unsalted butter
  • Milk
  • Vanilla extract
  • Vegetable oil
  1. Preheat oven to 350 F
  2. Line muffin tin
  3. Mix dry ingredients
  4. Heat milk-butter
  5. Mix eggs
  6. Add sugar to eggs
  7. Beat eggs until tripled
  8. Add dry mix into egg mixture slowly
  9. Temper milk
  10. Slowly add milk mixture
  11. Scrape and final mix
  12. Fill muffin tin
  13. Bake for 22 minutes
  14. Remove from oven and cool
  15. Frost cupcakes
